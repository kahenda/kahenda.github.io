01 — My Story

The Honest
Journey

I didn't grow up dreaming of being a developer. I didn't even know much about coding until a family member introduced me to Zone 01 Kisumu — a peer-to-peer learning school with no teachers, no lectures, just problem solving.

They had me play a cognitive game. I passed. That opened the door to a one-month intensive called Piscine — one of the hardest selection processes you'll ever face. Long hours, tough challenges, no hand-holding.

I passed that too. And now I'm one of the apprentices at Zone 01 Kisumu, doing my one-year module as a developer — building real things, pushing through real struggles.

My laptop broke mid-project. I lost code I hadn't pushed to GitHub. I started over. That's the job.
